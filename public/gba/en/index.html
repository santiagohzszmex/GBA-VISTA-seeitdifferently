03 / ANIMA · ONGOING RESEARCH

Accessible intelligence.
A human purpose.

ANIMA explores a familiar everyday presence: relevant conversation, continuity and memory under the person’s control. Accessibility means understanding the interaction, participating naturally and deciding how AI fits into one’s own life.

Current stage
We have a desktop prototype with verbatim retrieval, a deterministic router and reviewable actions. Contextual continuity and open-ended conversation remain research and validation goals.
01

Context before responses.

Study how to interpret each turn in relation to a previous question, proposal or situation. Clarify uncertainty and acknowledge actual limitations.

02

Understandable interaction.

Investigate how to make AI’s capabilities and limitations clear, adapt interaction to different needs and let people redirect a conversation.

03

Closeness with autonomy.

Explore consent-based memory that can be inspected, corrected and deleted, and adjustable initiative. An interaction that respects silence and life beyond the screen.

Discuss the research↗
How we distinguish an intention from a result

We separate technical integrity, conversational relevance, behavior and measured resources. A visible history is not semantic memory; passing a technical check does not demonstrate understanding. Proposed methods need a baseline comparison and independent validation. We do not claim consciousness, clinical capability or general understanding as ANIMA results.

04 / HOW WE BUILD

Judgment before promises.

01

People at the center.

A digital conversation can be valuable. It can also end when someone returns to their community, rests or continues with their day.

02

Evidence matters.

We distinguish hypotheses, prototypes and measured results. A visual demonstration does not replace an evaluation of usefulness or resources.

03

Build, share, care.

GBA Forge develops the tools and prototypes that give shape to our ideas. We assess design through people’s experience and results we can review.
